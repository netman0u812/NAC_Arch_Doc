Identity Hardening — DHCP Snooping + DAI

         [Client]
            |
         (DHCP)
            v
      [Access Switch]  -- DHCP Snooping -->  Binding Table (MAC,IP,VLAN,Port)
            |
          (ARP)
            v
      DAI checks ARP against Binding Table:
           +----------------------------------------+
           | If ARP matches binding → forward       |
           | If mismatch/spoof → drop + log (DAI)   |
           +----------------------------------------+

Result: MAC/IP identity used by NAC for per-client policy is protected from spoofing/poisoning.
